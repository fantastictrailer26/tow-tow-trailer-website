Measure the load and your storage space

Start with the largest item you plan to carry and the space you have for parking the trailer. The model names describe nominal sizes. Ask us for usable internal dimensions and overall dimensions, including the drawbar, if your load or storage area is a tight fit.

Match the weight as well as the size

A larger floor does not automatically mean a higher weight rating. All four listed models have a 750kg ATM. ATM includes the trailer and its load; ask us for the tare weight and available payload of the exact trailer and accessories you are considering.

Choose the accessories for your jobs

A 600mm or 900mm cage adds side height for bulky loads. For equipment, ask about ramp compatibility and dimensions. Browse spare wheels, ramps and storage accessories before requesting a complete quote.

Common questions before buying

Where can I view the trailers?

Visit Tow Tow Trailer at 87 Princes Hwy, Fairy Meadow NSW 2519, in the Wollongong area. We are open every day from 9am to noon. Call 0413 375 853 to check the model you want is available before travelling. See our map links and opening hours.

Does the 8x5 trailer price include the cage in the photos?

The 8x5 box trailer is listed at $1,999 excluding GST. The photos show a cage fitted; cages and other accessories are separate options. Ask us for a quote covering your chosen trailer and cage.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Tell us your preferred size, what you intend to carry, the approximate load weight, any cage or ramp requirements, and when you need the trailer. This helps us confirm a suitable setup and current stock.
